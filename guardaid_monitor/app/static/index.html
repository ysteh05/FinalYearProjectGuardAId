V

Violence & physical bullying

Fine-tuned MC3-18 · 4-second temporal-window model

MC3-18 · Kinetics-400 fine-tune · 16 frames

Current inference input: 4-second windows, 2-second stride, 16 RGB frames sampled at bin centres (4 FPS), resized proportionally to fit the full frame inside 160×160, then padded to 160×160 with the MC3 RGB mean before normalization (letterbox; no center crop). The threshold fields above control the active decision levels. Role sensitivity determines ordinary phone notifications. Changing window length, stride, or frame count departs from the checkpoint's training/calibration protocol and may change detection performance.

Unanswered alert notifications

Two-stage reminders for pending decisions or handlers

NEW ALERTS

The second delay must be later than the first. For an undecided alert, the first repeat goes to accounts that actually received the initial ordinary or VLM-supplemental notification; later eligible accounts retain the original phase timing. The second stage goes to all active users with a role and enabled device. After Confirm only, the first repeat also includes the selected broadcast roles (without an extra sensitivity filter); the second stage goes to all active role-bearing users with enabled devices. Dismissal, confirmation-and-handling, taking ownership, or resolution stops the relevant reminder phase. Opening/reading an alert, adding a comment, or recording a simulated emergency call does not stop it. Initial, first-repeat and all-user channels use two, three and four beeps respectively; routine decision updates use the normal notification channel. Timing is snapshotted for each phase.

M

MiniCPM assistance

Borderline review and incident scene descriptions

V4.7 · 1B · Q4_K_M

Scene descriptions use Ollama thinking; Borderline Review remains non-thinking. Frames retain their aspect ratio at a 360-pixel short side. Borderline review checks windows from Sensitive up to Certain until the first positive result for that alert, then skips further reviews. Ordinary threshold notifications continue independently. A violence scene description runs once after all windows eligible for the configured incident merge gap are processed, or when the video ends. Scenes up to 8 seconds use 2 FPS; longer scenes use 1 FPS. Only the first 60 seconds of the incident are described; full video evidence is retained.

D

Developer diagnostics

Time-aligned model score charts during video analysis

LOCAL DEBUG

When enabled, analyses started after saving show an MC3 Fight probability chart and a YOLO chart with one confidence line per weapon class in the active model. Click a time on either chart to inspect its nearest sample: MC3 opens that video window; YOLO shows the sampled frame and its bounding boxes, if any. Use +/− or scroll/pinch to zoom the X axis, Reset to show the full video, or right-drag to pan; the Y axis remains 0–100%. YOLO confidence is a detector score, not a calibrated probability. Red sample outlines mark alerts; yellow outlines mark threshold hits without a new alert. This display does not change detector decisions.
